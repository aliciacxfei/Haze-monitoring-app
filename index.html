Haze Watch

Haze readings (PSI, PM2.5 and PM10) cover Singapore only and come from the National Environment Agency (NEA) through data.gov.sg. Weather for every city comes from Open-Meteo.com.

Contains information from the Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) dataset accessed from data.gov.sg, made available under the terms of the Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0.
